# music-behavioral-analytics
Analyzing 2.1M+ Spotify chart entries across 73 countries to find what makes songs go global using SQL, Python, and Power BI
# Spotify Global Music Behavioral Analysis
**SQL Server | Python (Pandas) | Power BI | 2.1M+ Chart Entries | 73 Countries | 2024–2025**

---

## About This Project

I analyzed 2.1 million daily Spotify chart entries across 73 countries to understand how music actually travels the world.
Which artists dominate globally? What does a chart-topping song actually sound like? Do people in different countries really listen differently?

The analysis covers Artist Dominance, Song Longevity, Regional Mood Profiling, Audio Features, and Chart Behavior — with a Power BI dashboard on top.

---

## What I Found

- **73 countries** tracked — **13,664** unique artists across all charts
- **Billie Eilish** and **Bad Bunny** were the most featured artists globally
- True global reach is rare — very few artists hit **#1 in 50+ countries**
- Only **40.1%** of charted songs survive past 30 days — most disappear fast
- **Explicit songs** make up **67.3%** of all chart entries
- **El Salvador, Uruguay, and Turkey** consistently top the charts for energy and danceability
- Top 10 songs are noticeably more energetic and danceable than rank 50–100 — the audio formula is real
